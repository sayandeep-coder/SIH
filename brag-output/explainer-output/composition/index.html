02 · WHY
Why Build It?
Airfare is volatile — lead time, demand, seasonality and route all move the price. A manual survey can't keep up.
WHY AN INDEX
One fare is an observation. An index summarizes movement across a basket of routes and windows.
WHY T+1 → T+45
Airfare depends on how far in advance you price it — the windows capture booking lead time.
WHO BENEFITS
MoSPI / NSO, analysts and policymakers monitoring airfare for CPI-related analysis.
